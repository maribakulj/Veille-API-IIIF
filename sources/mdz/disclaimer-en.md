Disclaimer

Objects in the Digital Collections can be problematic from an ethical and moral point of view due to what they depict or their content. The Digital Collections display these objects in order to enable and promote a critical, sensitive examination of their representations and content. The Bavarian State Library, as the operator of the Digital Collections, expressly distances itself from discriminatory, racist, stereotyping, inhumane representations and content.
