Princeton University Library

Princeton University Library (PUL) is the main library system of Princeton University. With holdings of more than 7 million books, 6 million microforms, and 48,000 linear feet of manuscripts, it is among the largest libraries in the world by number of volumes. PUL has served as the main repository for high resolution, IIIF scans institutions without such hosting capabilities and provided guidance for digital archiving including workshops for metadata creation.
