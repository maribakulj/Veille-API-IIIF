 Be aware that maps and images originally published after 1929 may still be under copyright, and it is your responsibility to check copyright status on such images before using them. We ask that you make a good faith effort to contact the original publisher of such maps and images, or otherwise determine the copyright status of such maps and images before using them even after we grant permission to use our image file. Any maps and images that do not have a download button on them are not available for use other than display on our website. However if you are able to obtain usage permission from the estate or original publisher of the author we will allow use of our image file once we receive forwarded permission from the estate or original publisher. Original publishers and estate information can be found to the left of such maps and images on www.davidrumsey.com under "Media Information".
 Please give image credit to "David Rumsey Map Collection, David Rumsey Map Center, Stanford University Libraries"   For downloading images, use the Export button on the menu bar or use the Download 1 link at the bottom of the Media Information window. The Download 1 link will give a full resolution JP2 file.
