 National Library of Scotland AI Statement
 The Library’s AI statement sets out our approach towards AI and the principles that underpin it. 
 AI Statement
 As a national library we have a role to play as a trustworthy source of information, and as a responsible steward of collections and data. This requires us to think carefully about how and when new technology is used. In particular, we have been considering the potential impacts the rapidly evolving AI landscape could have on how we work, manage collections, and enable access to information.
Our AI Statement sets out our approach to the responsible adoption and use of artificial intelligence. It will guide our decision-making on how we employ AI tools, communicate about our use of AI, collect AI-generated material, and allow our data to be used by others.
