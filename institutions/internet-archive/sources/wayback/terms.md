> In particular, you certify that your use of any part of the Archive's Collections will be limited to noninfringing or fair use under copyright law.
> ... no other access to or use of the Site, the Collections, or the Archive's services is authorized.

Aucun chiffre de débit ni de quota dans ces conditions ; seule clause voisine : ne pas « interfere with ... servers, or resources ».
