 Browse or search titles from Digital Collections and its sub collections, such as Image Collections or Special Collections. Library Catalog main page default search also returns IIIF-enabled digital titles as well as physical and electronic records.
 IIIF viewer is embedded and displayed at each record level interface. Universal viewer is adopted for book titles, while mirador3 viewer displays images collection resources.
 Direct link to IIIF resource is displayed at record level under View Online section as: Full-screen view at: National Gallery of Art Library - Digital Collections
 Example record: Grandezze della città di Roma antiche e moderne come al presente si ritrovano, di nuovo ristampato in quattro linguaggi: latino, volgare, francese…

 International Image Interoperability Framework 
 IIIF is a set of open standards for delivering high-quality, attributed digital objects online at scale. It’s also an international community developing and implementing the IIIF APIs. IIIF is backed by a consortium of leading cultural institutions.
