 Copyright 
 The National Diet Library Search references multiple databases both inside and outside the National Diet Library when conducting a search. Copyrights of the data in the catalogs, indexes, digital archives, and other sources that are the subject of the search belong to the data providers. The use of individual data should be in accordance with the terms of use of the data provider. You can check the data provider in the List of Affiliated Databases . 
 In addition, the copyright of the site in the NDL Search belongs to the National Diet Library. Upon using the contents of this website, please comply with the Terms of Use of Contents of the National Diet Library's Website . 
 Link 
 You are free to link to pages from the NDL search, the following URL. 
 Please use https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/ to link to the NDL Search. 
 When linking to a page in the NDL Search, be sure to clearly indicate that the page is from the NDL Search. 
 You can use the following banner when linking. 
 About reprints 
 If you wish to reprint the contents of the NDL Search pages or the data provided by the National Diet Library, please refer to Using the Collections > Utilizing content from the National Diet Library website on the National Diet Library website. 
 Use of Bibliographic Data 
 Bibliographic data created by the National Diet Library 
 Bibliographic data created by the National Diet Library and downloaded from the NDL Search may be free of charge by anyone, with no restrictions on usage or purpose. For details on matters to keep in mind when using the site, please refer to the " Using Bibliographic Data (in Japanese) " on the NDL's website. 
 Bibliographic data of data providers 
 Copyrights of the data in the catalogs, indexes, digital archives and other sources that are the subject of the search belong to the data providers. The use of individual data should be in accordance with the terms of use of the data provider. For the terms of use of metadata via API, please refer to List of data providers provided by API .

 See Privacy Policy . 
 Web Accessibility 
 In order to ensure that all patrons can enjoy using the NDL search as well as access and use the information and functionality provided by the NDL's web services without hindrance irrespective of their physical condition, mental condition, or their environment, the National Diet Library has established Guidelines for Older Persons and Persons with Disabilities—Information and communications equipment, software and services—Part 3 : Web content(JIS X 8341—3:2010). We have also formulated the National Diet Library Web Accessibility Policy(in Japanese) and are striving to ensure and improve web accessibility.
