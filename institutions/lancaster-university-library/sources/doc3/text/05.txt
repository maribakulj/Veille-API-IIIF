 Research at Lancaster
 Research and the creation of new knowledge is central to Lancaster's role in generating global positive change. Excellent research informs our teaching, enterprise activity and engagement and underpins the University's status and reputation. Our first and guiding priority is to undertake research of the very highest quality by international standards, as recognised by our peers, funders, stakeholders, users and adopters.
