 Welcome to Lancaster Digital Collections
 Lancaster Digital Collections exists to showcase, promote and preserve unique and
 distinctive visual and multimedia digital collections from Lancaster University’s
 research outputs, Archives and Special Collections, and links with external partners.
 It enhances and benefits teaching, research and public engagement, and ensures that
 collections achieve maximum impact both within academia and in wider society.
 Following the launch of Lancaster Digital Collections in 2021, we will be developing
 and adding further collections on a regular basis.
 If you have any comments or questions about Lancaster Digital Collections please
 contact us .
