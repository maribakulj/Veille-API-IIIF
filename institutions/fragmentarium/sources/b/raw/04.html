Partners


Fragmentarium began as a community endeavour, as an agreement among representatives of manuscript institutions around the world to support a common laboratory for fragments. The success of Fragmentarium and its continued growth depend on healthy partnerships and the projects that they produce. We welcome inquiries concerning possible collaborations — contact us!

Founding Partners

In the first phase, Fragmentarium operated as a closed laboratory, and closely collaborated with sixteen partners to develop the technologies and techniques needed to conduct fragmentology on an international level.

  • Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich
  • Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, Vatican City
  • Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris, together with the BIBLISSIMA team for excellence
  • Bodleian Library, together with St. Edmund Hall, University of Oxford (Founding Partner)
  • Center for History and Palaeography – National Bank of Greece Cultural Foundation, Athens
  • Houghton Library, Harvard University, Cambridge MA, together with the Medieval Academy of America, Boston MA
  • Herzog-August-Bibliothek, Wolfenbüttel
  • Österreichische Nationalbibliothek, Vienna
  • Stanford University Libraries
  • Stiftsbibliothek St. Gallen
  • The British Library, London
  • The Schøyen Collection, Oslo and London
  • Università degli Studi di Cassino e del Lazio meridionale
  • University of Pennsylvania Libraries, Philadelphia
  • Universitätsbibliothek Leipzig
  • Yale University, New Haven
