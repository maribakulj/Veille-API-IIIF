Overview
The Media Presentation Service (MPS) provides infrastructure for IIIF asset delivery at Harvard. It is responsible for ingesting items from the Digital Repository Service marked as deliverable, and it provides space for campus partners to host and present objects and their accompanying IIIF manifests.
MPS replaced Harvard Library’s Image and Page Delivery Services in 2025.
