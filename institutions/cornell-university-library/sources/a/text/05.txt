 Welcome to the Digital Scholarship Guides site! Digital Scholarship Services is a department of Cornell University Library that supports research, teaching and consultations regarding digital scholarship and digital humanities projects. Learn more on the department website . Browse content to learn about digital tools, methods and resources for your research. Feel free to use the search bar in the left sidebar to find the content you’re looking for.
