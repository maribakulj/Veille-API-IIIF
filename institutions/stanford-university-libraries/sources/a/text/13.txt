 Contact us
 Chat with us online
 Libraries staff are here to help answer your questions. Available while the reference desk is open.
 Open chat window 
 Suggest a purchase
 If we don’t have an item in our collection, you can request it from a partner library via interlibrary loan . If you still can't find what you're looking for, you can suggest that the Libraries purchase an item. 
 Fill out the form to suggest a purchase .
 Submit a reference question
 Your question will be forwarded to the appropriate librarian for a response. We typically respond in one to two business days. 
 Submit a question to a reference librarian .
 Find a subject specialist
 We have over fifty subject specialists that can help you with your research. Whether you are new to Stanford or a seasoned researcher, we are here to help.
 Find a subject specialist .
 Email or call
 library-reference@stanford.edu 
 Reference desk (650) 725-1064

 Share your website feedback
 Tell us if you have any thoughts about our website.
 Share your feedback with the website team .
 Report a connection problem
 Having trouble accessing the catalog or another one of our online resources? Report your problem.
 Fill out the form to report a connection problem .
 Visit us in person
 Reference librarians are available in Green Library East, next to the circulation desk, Monday through Friday, 10 am to 5 pm.
 We can help you find materials or refer you to a subject specialist for more complex searches.
