 Takedown notice 
 Notice
 Durham University Library & Collections actively seeks to make content available online in digitised form to facilitate research, improve access to collections and help to preserve the originals.
 If you are a rights holder in material that has been made available online and wish to have that material removed from online access please contact us in writing at:
 Digital Special Collections Team
 Palace Green Library
 Palace Green
 Durham
 DH1 3RN
 Please include your contact details, an identification of the specific material concerned, the URL at which this material was found and a statement that you are or are the authorised representative of the rights holder.
 Procedure
 Upon receipt of a takedown request, the request will be acknowledged and the request and material concerned will be reviewed within a fortnight.
 In the case of a valid request the material will be temporarily removed from public access until an agreement is reached on how to resolve the issue.
 Until a mutually agreeable resolution has been reached the material in question will not be available from our servers.
