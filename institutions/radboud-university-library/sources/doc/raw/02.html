Browsing special collections

The Special Collections in the University Library include a large collection of unusual and rare books. The collection contains an extremely varied selection of manuscripts, old prints, incunabula, atlases, bibliophile editions and other printed works covering a wide range of subjects. These are mainly works produced before 1900. The collection is accessible to students, staff and external visitors in various ways.

The collection

Over the years, the University Library has compiled a large collection of special books through purchases and donations. The collection is remarkably diverse in nature. Here is a selection of the subject areas:

  • Natural history
  • Medicine
  • Physics and mathematics
  • Engineering
  • Plays
  • Freemasonry
  • Language and literature
  • Philosophy and theology
  • Law

Browsing the collection

Items from the Special Collections can be consulted in person in the Heritage Collections Reading Room (UBN.1.05D) at the University Library. As a student or staff member, you can use your Radboud account to request items from the Special Collections via RUQuest. You can find more information about the search process on the ‘Search for literature’ page.

Request materials via RUQuest

If you do not work at university or you would like to browse a work that cannot be requested via RUQuest, please complete the ‘Request Special Collections’ form. The University Library staff will contact about your request within two days.

Browsing the collection digitally

Part of the Special Collections can be viewed digitally. Not all works from the Special Collections – which comprise tens of thousands of titles in total – have been digitised. Works that have already been digitised can be accessed via the relevant title descriptions in the RUQuest search system by clicking the ‘Online access’ button. Furthermore, the digitised works are organised by theme in the digital image bank.

Digital collections

If you have any questions regarding the content of the Special Collections, please submit them via Ask Your Librarian. Questions are normally answered by a curator within five working days.

Digitising collections

If you would like to have a work from the Special Collections digitised, please contact the University Library’s studiezaalerfgoed [at] ru.nl (subject: Request%20Digitising%20Special%20Collections) (Heritage Collections Reading Room). Please make sure that your request contains the following information:

  • First name and surname
  • Title and signature of the work that needs to be digitised
  • Page numbers
  • Whether you would like to have the work digitised for publication or for your own use

You will receive a response within five days. Not all works can be digitised.

Collections in education?

Are you a lecturer and would you like to incorporate works from the Special Collections into your lectures? Please contact the curators at least two months in advance via bijzonderecollecties [at] ru.nl (bijzonderecollecties[at]ru[dot]nl). They will be happy to advise you on which works are suitable for the specific course you are teaching. The curators can often also act as guest speakers to talk more about the collection and the physical nature of books. If arranged in good time, it is possible to hold the session in the Heritage Collections Reading Room (UBN.1.05D).

Donating books?

Are you considering donating a book to the Special Collections? Please send an email to bijzondere.collecties [at] ru.nl (bijzondere[dot]collecties[at]ru[dot]nl) with a brief description of the book, preferably including photographs and any relevant details. Our curators will carefully review your offer and then contact you as soon as possible.

Making a financial contribution?

If you would like to support the Special Collections financially, you can make a contribution

via the Radboud Fund. You can do this through a one-off or regular donation, or via a bequest, in which case you can earmark your gift for the Special Collections. Further information can be found on the ‘donate’ and ‘bequests’ pages of the Radboud Fund’s website. The Radboud Fund would be happy to discuss the options with you and can be contacted at info [at] radboudfonds.nl (info[at]radboudfonds[dot]nl) or by telephone on +31 24 36201 56.

 

Would you like to work with items from the Special Collections whilst you’re studying, for example by doing an internship or writing a thesis? Please contact the curators at bijzonderecollectie [at] ru.nl (bijzonderecollectie[at]ru[dot]nl). They’ll be happy to help you!
