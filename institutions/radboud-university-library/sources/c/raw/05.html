Sharing research results in the Radboud Repository

The Radboud Repository is the digital storage place for sharing your research results as a Radboud researcher so that other people can also see them. For all publications from Radboud University, the university aims to make them publicly accessible in the Radboud Repository whenever possible. As a scientist, you can do this yourself in most cases. For dissertations, this is mandatory.

The advantages of sharing through the Radboud Repository

  • your research is available to everyone
  • your research is freely accessible and easy to find
  • Google (Scholar) and other search engines rank Radboud Repository higher in the search results, because they classify the content as scientifically reliable
  • The full text of the publications is fully searchable, leading to better findability in Google (Scholar)
  • Better findability of research results leads to more citations of your publications
  • from within the Radboud Repository you can download research results and share them with other people by email and on social media
  • the complete publication texts in the Repository are archived sustainably in the national e-Depot of the national library.
  • Publications from the Radboud Repository can be found in the Dutch version of research portal Openaire.
  • It is possible to link your publications to the corresponding datasets. And with your datasets in the Radboud Repository, you will see what the corresponding publication is.

Radboud Repository

Submitting

Uploading your publication (with the exception of dissertations) can be done in two ways:

  • Via Research Information Services (RIS)
  • Directly in the Radboud Repository

After uploading, there is always a check whether the publication may be disclosed according to copyright law.

Submitting dissertation

Is the publication a dissertation? Then a different procedure applies.

PhD students are obliged to publish their dissertation in the Radboud Repository. A provision in the doctoral regulations states that you have to make your dissertation available open access through the Radboud Repository, the day after your public defense.

Postponing publication of dissertation

It is possible to request permission from the Chairman of the PhD Board to postpone publication. Complete the "Request for embargo on the digital dissertation" form. You have received this form from the beadle. Send a scan along with evidence two months before the promotion date to repository [at] ubn.ru.nl. 

Important: an embargo is only permitted in exceptional circumstances.

Submitting the dissertation

Submit the text as a single file in PDF format. If your thesis appears in print, the digital file should be identical to the printed version (on account of page references and indexes). If a commercial version of the manuscript is available that is different from the one approved by the manuscript committee, we strongly recommend that you do not submit the commercial version without approval from the publisher. You can then submit the version of the manuscript that has been approved by the manuscript committee. Providing a printed version of the manuscript is not necessary.

Sending an English summary or description of your dissertation (max. 300 words) along with your PDF will increase the visibility (in Google, for example).

Send the digital version to repository [at] ubn.ru.nl (repository[at]ubn[dot]ru[dot]nl). For large files, use Filesender.

Please note that if you have your dissertation published at Radboud University Press, Radboud University Press will provide your dissertation.

Allowance for printing costs

On receipt of the digital copy of the dissertation, you will be sent a confirmation of receipt which you can use to declare the printing costs. See also the printing costs reimbursement regulations.

Link to dissertation

After the promotion, you will receive a link to your dissertation in the Radboud Repository. If you would like to have the link beforehand, you can request it through repository [at] ubn.ru.nl (repository[at]ubn[dot]ru[dot]nl).
