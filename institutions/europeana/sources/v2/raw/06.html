Europe's cultural heritage, via APIs

Search, display and use millions of digitised artworks, newspapers, photographs and more from thousands of institutions across Europe.

Get your free API key

To request an API key, you’ll need a free Europeana account. This gives you a central place to view/manage your API keys, and gives Europeana a stable contact for support and sharing important updates.

What you can access with the APIs

Thousands of European cultural heritage institutions have digitised and made their collections available through Europeana: from books and manuscripts, to artworks and even videos and 3D objects. Explore some of our popular collections below with the Europeana website, which uses the APIs.

3M+ artworks

From well-known works to hidden gems, the Europeana collections house millions of beautiful artworks.

6M+ photographs

Photographs spanning centuries, from major national collections to small regional archives, ready for you to use.

2M+ newspapers

Search centuries of headlines across a vast newspaper archive, with millions of pages available as searchable full text.

12M+ natural history items

A growing collection of plants, animals, and other natural history specimens with rich metadata.

What you can do with the APIs

Explore how to use Europeana data in different contexts. Pick the path that sounds like you.

Build an app or service

Search the collections, pull records, and display high-resolution images in a IIIF viewer. Powered by the Search, Record and IIIF APIs.

Teach at university level

Give every student a free personal key and build classroom exercises on real cultural heritage data. Well-paired with our self-led Europeana Academy training course.

Research or analyse the collections

Query millions of items and export what you need to run large text and metadata analysis. Powered by SPARQL, OAI-PMH, and dataset downloads.

Access and use data at scale

Harvest full datasets and run high-volume services with the throughput a production project needs. Powered by OAI-PMH.

Built with our APIs

From virtual galleries to music search engines, developers, researchers and educators are already building with our APIs.

Fornland

Explore over 1.6 million archaeological sites, historic monuments and cultural landscapes from across the Nordic and Celtic world into a single interactive map. Each location is enriched with historical images, documents and artefacts powered by the Europeana APIs.

Go to Fornland (opens in new window)

Craft Atlas

The Craft Atlas is an open-access platform for exploring craft knowledge, techniques and makers. By integrating Europeana’s collections through the Europeana APIs, the platform connects craft techniques with historical objects, images and cultural heritage records.
