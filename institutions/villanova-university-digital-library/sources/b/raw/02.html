Distinctive Collections: Digital Library

Digital Library

Overview

Falvey's Digital Library assembles, presents, and preserves digital collections that support the teaching and research of the campus and the global community of scholars drawing materials from Villanova's University Archives, Special Collections, and a host of external Digital Partners. Ranging from digitization of Villanova Dissertations and Theses, to digital preservation of faculty full text publications, the Digital Library actively solicits project ideas and materials for digitization from community members; please contact the Digital Library Team with your idea for a new digital project. We work with faculty, staff and regional partners to select and develop resources that enhance access for a wide range of disciplines and audiences.

Digital Scholarship Services

Digital Scholarship Lab

Service Overview

Falvey Library's Digital Scholarship Program provides a number of services to Villanova faculty, staff, and students to support digital research, teaching, and learning. From designing a digital humanities project to creating a blog or data visualization, we're here to help.

See our list of workshops to see what trainings are currently being offered. We also offer workshops available on request or contact us to discuss putting together a custom workshop.
