Digital Scholarship Services

Work with us

Falvey Library’s Digital Scholarship Program provides a number of services to Villanova faculty, staff, and students to support digital research, teaching, and learning. From designing a digital humanities project to creating a blog, podcast or data visualization, we’re here to help. Some of our main areas of support include:

  • GIS Mapping
  • Digital Exhibits
  • Text and Data Mining
  • Web Archiving
  • Digital Publishing
  • Data Visualization
  • Online Research Visibility
  • Open Educational Resources

If you would like help in an area not listed above, we may still be able to help you. Contact Us.

Consultations

We're available to help you get started with a new digital project, integrate a digital assignment into your course, or learn more about a particular software, tool, or method. We can meet with you to discuss your digital research and teaching needs and how Falvey Library may be able to collaborate with you or otherwise provide support. Contact us to schedule a consultation.

Workshops and trainings

The Digital Scholarship Program hosts workshops and trainings throughout the year. See our list of workshops to see what trainings are currently being offered. We also offer customized workshops and instruction for your group or class. Choose from our list of workshops available on request or contact us to discuss putting together a custom workshop.

Digital Scholarship Lab and Spaces

Following its recent move to Falvey Library at Vic Maggitti Hall, the Digital Scholarship Lab provides Villanova faculty, staff, and students with access to technology, creative spaces, and services that support digital scholarship, teaching, learning, research, and multimedia production. Located in G01, the Lab is a collaborative environment for exploring digital humanities, digital media, immersive technologies, data-intensive research, and emerging forms of scholarship. As we continue to develop our new spaces and services, additional technologies, resources, and reservation options will be introduced.

See Digital Scholarship Lab for more information.

Multimedia Equipment Loans

The Multimedia Equipment Loan Program provides Villanova faculty, staff, and students with access to multimedia equipment for academic, research, and creative projects.

Equipment may be checked out from the library's service desk for up to 7 days, with no renewals.

See Multimedia Equipment Loans for more information.

Digital projects

Need help getting started on a digital project? Have a digital project in mind that you would like to partner with Falvey Library on? Fill out our project proposal form or contact us, so we can assist you with figuring out which digital tools, software, and hardware are best for your project. For more information, see Start a Digital Project.
