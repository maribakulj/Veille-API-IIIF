 Request Digitization
 Before you submit a request, search Yale Library Digital Collections to see if there are items that have already been digitized to support your research (see the note under “Delivery and Turnaround Time” about existing audiovisual materials in Aviary). You must be registered with a Yale Library Special Collections ("Aeon") Account to submit a digitization request.
 How to Request from Library Catalogs 
 Find the item you want in one of the Yale Library catalogs (Archives at Yale or Quicksearch). The steps for requesting images vary slightly between catalogs. For additional help, contact repository staff.  
 Request from Archives at Yale 
 Find and select the item you are interested in and click “Request.”  
 Click “Continue” (the "Type of request" dropdown will say "Reading Room").   
 Log in. 
 In the list of Unsubmitted Requests, find the item and click “Request Digitization.” 
 Scroll through the Digital Copy Request form, make a selection in the "Format" dropdown (PDF, TIFF, MP3, or MP4), add a page count or plate count as appropriate, add any important information about your order in the “Notes” field, and click “Submit Request.” 
 Request from Quicksearch   
 In the item record, click on the link that says, “Request for use in ______ (repository)”   
 Log in. 
 Scroll down to the New Request form and click “Switch to Digital Copy Request.”  
 Scroll through the Digital Copy Request form, make a selection in the "Format" dropdown (PDF, TIFF, MP3, or MP4), add a page count or plate count as appropriate, add any important information about your order in the “Notes” field, and click “Submit Request.”  
 Order Limits and Formats 
 There are no charges for the services listed below.
 Order Limits 
 Book, non-illuminated: Up to 2 volumes, no more than 600 pages total
 Oversize Items (e.g. maps, posters, blueprints - larger than 24 x 18 inches): 15 items from one container (e.g., box, flat file, portfolio)
 Unbound Documents: Up to 4 folders
 Pamphlets: Up to 4 pamphlets, no more than 600 pages total
 Contact sheets, slides, and negatives:
 a. Contact sheets from up to 4 folders will be provided for reference/selection purposes (note: if there are no physical contact sheets in the folder, photographers will create high resolution digital contact sheets)
 b. Individual slides or negatives: 50 from up to 4 folders
 Glass plate slides and lantern slides: Up to 50 slides from one box
 Audio-visual: 4 items*  
 Service Formats 
 PDF - 300 ppi 
 TIFF - between 400-600 ppi 24 bit RGB 
 Sound Recording Streaming MP3 256kps 
 Video Recording Streaming MP4 H.264 5.0mps AAC 256kps
 Delivery and Turnaround Time 
 Delivery 
 Files will be delivered electronically using a file transfer service called MASV and may arrive in multiple transfers over time. 
 Copies of materials that are already in digital form (born digital), will be provided in their native format and delivered electronically using a file transfer service called MASV.
 For sound and video recordings, patrons will be provided streaming access for thirty days via Aviary.  
 Turnaround time (please note that we do not accommodate rush orders, and that the below estimates are general guidelines and may vary depending on which library holds the requested material)
 Documents: 10-14 weeks    
 AV items: 8-10 weeks 
 * Audiovisual materials are digitized according to Yale Library's media preservation reformatting guidelines. Yale Library’s digitized audiovisual collections are available in Aviary. Patrons are encouraged to check there before placing their order. If the materials requested are already available digitally but are not available in Aviary, you will be contacted with an offer to stream the recordings for 30 days, free of charge.
