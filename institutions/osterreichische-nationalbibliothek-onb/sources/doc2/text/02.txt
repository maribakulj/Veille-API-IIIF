 Use of content made available on Austrian National Library websites
 The Austrian National Library makes content or digital copies of its holdings available online on its websites. The Austrian National Library does not assert any copyright user rights of its own to this content. To this extent, it also expressly agrees to the subsequent use of this content in the resolution retrievable from the web. This also applies to the use of this content in online forums, blogs and social media channels such as Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest etc. Irrespective of this, the user must clarify any existing third-party rights to the content individually before any subsequent use.
 For scans created by the cooperation partner Google, this authorisation for subsequent use applies with the restriction that the scans may be used for a limited period of time and only for non-commercial purposes. Information about the end of this period can be accessed together with the object in question. Enquiries regarding commercial uses of reproductions of these holdings during this period will be answered by our information service via information[at]onb.ac.at .
 When using our content, we request that you provide a picture credit (title, date and other available descriptions) and a source reference (citation link from [Portal] and the reference "[Portal]/Austrian National Library"). For postings on Facebook or Instagram, we request a link to the corresponding Austrian National Library account.
 Disclaimer of liability
 The information on this website has been compiled to the best of our knowledge and belief. Despite all due care, however, we cannot guarantee that the information provided here is absolutely accurate, up-to-date and correct. If we become aware of any errors or mistakes, we will endeavour to correct them. Contributions identified by name reflect the personal opinion of the respective author - the Austrian National Library accepts no responsibility for these contributions unless it has actual knowledge of illegal content and, as far as concerns claims for damages, unless it is aware of any facts or circumstances from which illegal activity or information is evident.
 If you become aware of illegal content, please contact us by e-mail at  feedback[at]onb.ac.at . After checking the situation, we will immediately endeavour to remove any such content from our website. 
 Hyperlinks
 We have included on our website links to other Internet sites. For all these links, we have no influence on the form and content of the linked pages. We therefore expressly distance ourselves from the content of these linked pages and do not adopt them as our own. This applies to the content of all external websites that can be accessed via our website (including via banners, guest books or counters).

 Our website uses cookies: in addition to those that are technically necessary for the functionality of our website and therefore cannot be rejected, we also use those whose purpose is to analyse website accesses, as well as those set by third-party providers such as Youtube and Google. With your consent to analysis and third-party cookies, data is processed both by us and by third-party providers, some of which are based in third countries (e.g. the USA). You can find more information about the tools and the partner:s in our data protection declaration. If you wish, you can adjust your cookie settings individually at any time via the link in the footer.
