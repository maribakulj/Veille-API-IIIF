The project “Simple Access to Cultural Heritage Assets” (SACHA) is a joint venture with the Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities (ACDH) of the Austrian Academy of Sciences. It aims to provide scientists easier access to data of importance for cultural studies.

You can use this IIIF interface to view images and create collections.
