Unique, valuable and rare early printed works, manuscripts, maps, globes, sheet music and other special materials can be found at the Department of Special Collections of Utrecht University Library. Curators, information specialists and other experts in the field of Special Collections are present to advise and support researchers, lecturers and students in their research, teaching and study activities.

You are welcome, both online and in the library.

Advice and support

  • Students

    Do you need help with study assignments, advice on your thesis or do you want to request scans? Special Collections will help you on your way.

    Advice and support to students
  • Researchers

    Do you know that our collection is digitally available for research, using innovative techniques? Let us know if you want to find out more.

    Advice and support to researchers
  • Lecturers

    Would you like to give a lecture at Special Collections? Or do you want advice on how to use special material in your teaching? Ask our experts.
