Datasets by OAI-PMH

NLP datasets are available by OAI-PMH (Open Archives Initiative - Protocol for Metadata Harvesting) in Dublin Core, MarcXchange, Mods, EDM and ESE. For several international portals, data contributed by the NLP is harvested with this service.

OAI-PMH access:
https://oai.bnportugal.gov.pt
