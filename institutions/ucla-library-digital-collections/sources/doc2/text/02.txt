 About this guide
 Library Digital Collections are relative newcomers to college and research libraries, historically speaking. This interactive guide introduces the wide variety of primary source content made available in digital repositories both at UCLA and around the world.
 Unlike standard digital library assets such as commercial ebooks or online scholarly journals, digital collections are composed of digitized copies of rare, unique items held in Special Collections or specialized institutional archives. These curated repositories offer much more than static online facsimiles of physical holdings; they are built to integrate with robust digital tools designed for interactive instruction, advanced research, and digital scholarly communication.
 Because the technologies powering digital collections are dynamic and evolving, this guide maps out the types of materials you will encounter and provides direct methods to help you effectively analyze, cite, and engage them in your academic research.
