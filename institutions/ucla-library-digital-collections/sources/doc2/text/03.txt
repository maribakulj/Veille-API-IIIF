 Sample Items
 UCLA Library Digital Collections provide access to hundreds of thousands of items. Here is a sample of four items to show the diversity of materials you might find:
 A Cuban cinema poster
 "15 Aniversario Cinemateca de Cuba" (1975) from Carteles de Cine , an International Digital Ephemera Project .
 An illuminated manuscript
 Manuscript No. 1: Gladzor Gospels (1300-1307) from Armenian Manuscripts 
 A news photograph
 "Impounded Japanese fishing boats, Fish Harbor, Los Angeles" (1942). Original photonegative from The Los Angeles Times Photographic Collection 
 A fashion design sketch
 Cashin's hand-painted illustrations of ensembles featuring black and white / gray Forstmann wool. f07-10 (Oct. 1953) from Cashin (Bonnie) Collection of Fashion, Theater and Film Costume Design, 1913-2000
