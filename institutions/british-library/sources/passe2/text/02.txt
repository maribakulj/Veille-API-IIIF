 Collection metadata services
 We offer a range of metadata services to libraries, publishers, researchers and the cultural heritage sector.
 The British National Bibliography (BNB)
 The British National Bibliography (BNB) records the publishing activity of the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland since 1950.
 The BNB is currently available on the Share Family beta portal . It is published as linked data as part of an international collaboration of libraries to enhance discovery.
 To request a Catalogue-in-Publication (CIP) record, please contact Bibliographic Data Services (BDS) .
 To request a weekly PDF of new additions to the BNB, please contact us at metadata@bl.uk .
 Z39.50 MARC 21 free data service
 Our Z39.50 MARC 21 service is available on an authenticated (i.e. password protected) basis to libraries wishing to use British Library MARC 21 records for non-commercial purposes. Once registered, you can download MARC 21 data from the British Library's catalogue and the British National Bibliography.
 Please contact metadata@bl.uk for more information about the terms and conditions of this service. If these are accepted, we will provide you with a username and password, along with some setup guidance.
 International Standard Name Identifier (ISNI)
 The British Library is an ISNI Registration Agency. We are working with partners in the cultural, research, publishing and music communities to assign ISNIs to persons and organisations/groups. To enquire about bulk data ISNI assignments or any other ISNI-related matter, please contact us at isni@bl.uk .
 International Standard Serial Number (ISSN)
 The British Library hosts ISSN UK, the national centre responsible for assigning ISSN to serials published in the United Kingdom.
 If you are a UK publisher and wish to request an application form, or have any questions about ISSN, please contact us at issn-uk@bl.uk .
 For non-UK publishers, please refer to the ISSN International Centre .
 MARC organization code and ISIL
 The British Library is the national agency for the assignment of MARC organization codes and International Standard Identifier for Libraries and related organizations (ISILs). Both identifiers can be assigned to libraries and related organisations, such as archives and museums located in the UK, the Crown Dependencies and Overseas Territories.
 If you are such an organisation and require a MARC organization code or an ISIL, please contact us at uk-isilmarc@bl.uk .

 Metadata standards
 The British Library is involved in the development, maintenance and / or implementation of professional metadata standards such as MARC 21 , RDA , DDC , FAST , (including the FAST funnel ). Any questions? Please contact us at metadata@bl.uk .
 Collection metadata strategy
 Sustainability through change (PDF, 853.9KB) is the British Library's collection metadata strategy, 2023-2030. It sets out three priorities: delivering core purposes, opening our metadata and removing barriers to access, and enabling our people.
 The strategy is implemented through annual plans, which allow for some reprioritisation of activities depending on latest developments.
 Open metadata
 As part of its collection metadata strategy, the British Library has reaffirmed its commitment to sharing its metadata openly with its user communities.
 This policy confirms the terms of licence, limitations and exceptions for sharing the British Library's open metadata.
 Researcher Format (csv) datasets are available from the British Library Research Repository.
