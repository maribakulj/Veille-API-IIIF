Our collection

Our shelves hold over 170 million items – a living collection that gets bigger every day.

We have millions of books, but also newspapers, maps, sound recordings, photographs, patents and stamps.

Search our available catalogues below. If you need tips or guidance on searching the catalogues, requesting items or understanding the different areas of our collection, explore our research guides.

You'll need a Reader Pass to see material in our Reading Rooms. You can find out how to use the Library here.

Explore our available catalogues

Main catalogue

The main British Library catalogue, including books, printed material, and some electronic resources.

Archives and Manuscripts Catalogue

An interim catalogue containing archives, manuscripts and visual arts collections.

E-Theses Online Service (EThOS)

An online platform of over 650,000 metadata records of UK doctoral theses.

Sound and Moving Image catalogue

This interim catalogue includes records from our sound and moving image collection.

Electronic Legal Deposit Catalogue

An interim catalogue for digital journals and books published in the UK. This catalogue contains records created before October 2023.

British National Bibliography (BNB)

Linked open data for books and journals published or distributed in the United Kingdom and Ireland since 1950, and electronic publications since 2013.

English Short Title Catalogue (ESTC)

Over 500,000 records of works printed in the English-speaking world before 1801. Hosted by the Consortium of European Research Libraries (CERL).

Incunabula Short Title Catalogue (ISTC)

The international database of 15th-century European printing, created by the British Library with contributions from institutions worldwide.

Digitised manuscripts and archives

View thousands of digitised manuscripts and archives, including the Sherborne Missal and Octateuch, Four Gospels and Synodicon.

Endangered Archives Programme (EAP)

Digitised archives from around the world that were in danger of destruction, neglect or physical deterioration.

Research

International Dunhuang Programme (IDP)

Over 40,000 conserved and digitised manuscripts, paintings, historical photographs, textiles and artefacts from the Eastern Silk Roads.

Research

Qatar Digital Library

Over two million digitised pages, covering the history and culture of the Gulf and wider region, including Arabic scientific manuscripts from our collection.

Research

Research Repository

An open access repository for outputs from our staff, data sets, 3D models of collection items, and content from other heritage organisations.

Research

Collection images

Our picture library gives instant access to thousands of the greatest images from our collection. Free and paid-for options, depending on resolution and copyright.

Google Books

Over 700,000 digitised books from our collection are available on Google Books.

Theatrical playbills

Around 80,000 theatre posters, including high resolution images with searchable text of English, Scottish, and Irish playbills made between 1600 and 1902.
