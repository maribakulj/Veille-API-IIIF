 About
 AMS Historica is the repository dedicated to the digitization of artworks, monuments, archaeological finds, manuscript codes, papyri, books, magazines, newspapers, maps, drawings, photographs, audio and video resources, and any other work of artistic, historical, archaeological, ethnological, anthropological, archival, and bibliographic interest preserved in the libraries, museums, and archives of the University of Bologna, or resulting from national and international research projects. In line with the University Policy on Digital Cultural Heritage , AMS Historica ensures compliance with national and international guidelines and standards that promote long-term preservation and the enhancement of digitized works. The contents of this repository are indexed by Cultura Italia, Europeana, OpenAIRE, WorldCat and BASE. Digitized works are published with usage licenses that encourage discovery, study, sharing, and reuse in accordance with the principles of Open Science. The usage licenses and service delivery methods are detailed on the Service Terms and Conditions page. For information on how to access the service, please refer to the dedicated page on the University of Bologna's Library System website.
 AlmaDL offers professional assistance to the research teams, the museums, the archives and the libraries of the University of Bologna involved in digitization projects of cultural, historical and scientific interest. The digital contents published in AMS Historica repository originate from initiatives of different disciplinary domains supporting research, teaching and learning or the promotion of cultural heritage. The deposit in AMS Historica repository guarantees preservation, visibility and access to multi-domain digital contents — texts, audio, video, images — according to national and international standards and thanks to the use of open formats and interoperability protocols. The open source platform used for the repository, DSpace-GLAM, integrates DSpace technology and IIIF ecosystems providing features and browsing modes that are particularly effective for viewing and studying digitized contents. AlmaDL staff offers support for:
 analysis of the original materials;
 data management plan;
 digitization and image processing;
 data model and metadata;
 legal guidance and support;
 archiving and publication in AMS Historica;
 discovery and dissemination.
 Please, contact the AlmaDL staff for further information: historica@unibo.it
 AMS Historica is a repository operated by AlmaDL , the digital library of the University of Bologna. AlmaDL collects, preserves and provides access to digital collections supporting institutional research, teaching and learning, and the promotion and valorization of cultural heritage. For more information see AlmaDL website.
