 Dynamic Examples
 Dynamic examples of using the JSON/YAML API for loc.gov
 On Monday, June 30, 2025, the Library of Congress launched a new online catalog platform. For general information, visit the Catalog Welcome Page . Updates are being restored incrementally to data available through the loc.gov JSON/YAML API, sitemaps, and various microservices. Data from these services are not fully synced to data from the catalog.
