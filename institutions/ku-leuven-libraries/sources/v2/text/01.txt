 Digitisation
 Introduction
 Digitisation at KU Leuven Libraries supports and stimulates research, education and heritage activities at KU Leuven, based on its expertise in the fields of project management, legal issues and digital imaging of analogue objects. Collection items are digitised with state-of-the-art infrastructure in both the Imaging Lab and the Scan Hub as part of the libraries' digitisation programme, in the framework of digitisation projects or based on digitisation requests.
 Together with the metadata specialists of  Digitisation and Enrichment of Collections  and our partner  LIBIS , we offer solutions for a wide spectrum of digitisation questions, ranging from the valorisation of collections to the enrichment of digitised materials for research purposes. Collection curators, researchers and teaching staff may contact us with their questions and ideas.
 Digitisation also offers bespoke services to organisations outside of KU Leuven.

 Digitisation
 Digitisation is the conversion of physical objects to digital data. These data can take many forms (e.g. image files, metadata, OCR data or multispectral data) and may serve as the basis for a large variety of actions.
 Digitisation is a multifaceted proces, including the physical preparation of the originals, metadating, imaging and other data creation, data preservation and publishing.
The specification of each of these facets depends on the digitisation objectives.  Digitisation coordinates the proces and is responsible for the project management.
 Digitisation projects are executed both as part of KU Leuven Libraries' collection policy and within the framework of research projects or education.
 For more information about our digitisation processes, you can check out our service catalogue  (in Dutch).
 VIEW
 Digitisation and Enrichment of Collections is an active member of the international research and development community in the field of digitisation, with a strong focus on scientific imaging techniques: colour callibration, multispectral imaging, multilight reflectance and 3D. Examples of R&D projects are​​​​  RICH ,  Fingerprint and Pixel+ .

 Network and international standards
 Our team follows the latest developments in the field with regards to technical aspects and concerning digitisation project management and legal matters. For that purpose, we take part in national and international panels, collaborations and conferences. We are, amongst others, a member of the ​​​​​​ Impact Centre of Competence in Digitisation , AHFAP  and the international GLAM Labs community . Our network includes  Metamorfoze , Cambridge University Library , British Library and the J ohn Rylands Library . For several projects, we collaborate with the Royal Library of Belgium and the Université Catholique de Louvain , and we are a member of the HERKUL , the KU Leuven Institute for Cultural Heritage.
 We participate in the development of best practise guidelines, e.g. a manual for the digitisation of manuscripts , and implement standards such as  Metamorfoze and Fadgi .
