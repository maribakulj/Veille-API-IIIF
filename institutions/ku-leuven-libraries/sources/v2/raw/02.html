The rights to the images shown vary:

- Images of works from the collections of KU Leuven Libraries may be freely downloaded and reused if these works are part of the public domain and provided that the library or collection that holds the original is mentioned. KU Leuven assumes that works published before 1900 belong to the public domain.

- Images of works published after 1900 should be used with caution. The correct observance of copyright regulations is the responsibility of the user of the images.

- Images of works that are not the property of KU Leuven are only used on this website if they belong to the public domain or with the permission of the owners. In the latter case, please contact the owner directly if you wish to use them.

Questions or comments about the use of the images on this site can be sent to us via LCS Digitisation [Link: digitisation@kuleuven.be].

The quality of the images is adapted to use for the web. For higher quality images, please visit the digital collection of the holding institution of the original. For KU Leuven Libraries, the digitised collections may be consulted via www.limo.libis.be.
