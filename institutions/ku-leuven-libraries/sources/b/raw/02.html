    Images as Open Data

    What 

    KU Leuven Libraries offers the available data that was created through digitisation of public domain library materials to the public as open data. More specifically, this concerns the consultation copies of those items from the KU Leuven Libraries collection that date from before 1901.

    Everyone interested may download and freely use these images. 

    Guidelines

    • Check the judicial status of a collection item in the Limo description.
    • Download the desired images in the Limo/Teneo viewer.
    • Use the images as you wish. We kindly ask you to mention 'KU Leuven' and the collection holder as owner of the original, as a token of appreciation for their work.
    • Name the holding library and shelfmark if possible.

    Furthermore…

    • Read the conditions for the use of the digitised collections of KU Leuven Libraries.
    • If you need a higher resolution image, please contact digitisation@kuleuven.be.
    • In case you need a collection item that has not yet been digitised, you can use our scan on demand service at a charge.
    • Interested in learning more about the steps we took to offer our images as open data? Read all about it in our blogpost.
    • Everything you need to know about Public Domain
    • More information regarding the FAIR data policy at KU Leuven.
