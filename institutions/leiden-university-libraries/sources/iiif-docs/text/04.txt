 Getting started with IIIF
 In many situations you don't even need to know that you are using IIIF.
If all goes well, you can view high-resolution images with annotations
in a viewer or other environment and get on with your work.
 These pages aim to introduce some concepts and tools so that you can
do more with images, annotations and manifests , i.e. so that you can
go beyond the viewer.
