 “文机談” from Kikutei Collection digitalized
 Bunkidan (文机談) is a collection of musical stories centered around the biwa (Japanese lute), compiled during the Bun'ei period (1264-1275). The manuscript of Kikutei Collection is believed to be a transcription from the late Kamakura period.

 Kitaro Nishida's donated books digitalized
 Japanese philosopher Kitaro Nishida donated six books to the library of the Third Higher School (currently Yoshida-South Library of Kyoto University) in memory of his deceased son Ken. Three of the books have photos of Ken and six tanka poems written by Nishida.

 Would you become a contributor to our projects?
 Rare materials, fragile through time, are usually restricted to secure their preservation. However, if they are digitized and available on the internet, we can learn the wisdom of our ancestors described there even at home. Still, many of them are waiting to be digitized, because the process requires so much care and cost. Your support through our fund would be highly appreciated.

 Nakai Collection - Kyoto-related treasures
 Nakai Collection, passed down in the Nakai family, a Tokugawa Government's master carpenter family in Kyoto, includes the plans of Nijo Castle, Kyoto Imperial Palace, Shimogamo Shrine and other shrines and temples, related documents or maps.
 Go to the Collection 
 Otogi Zoshi - outlines with colored illustrations
 Kyoto University holds 136 items (90 titles) of Otogi Zoshi, flamboyantly illustrated fairy tale books. So that you can taste the same enjoyment as people in the old days, here are their simplified outlines with beautifully colored illustrations in a picture book style.
 Go to the Pick Up 
 Meiji Restoration Collection
 A collection of materials relating the Meiji Restoration is available here. Formerly held by Sonjo-do founded by Yajiro Shinagawa in accordance with the wish of Shoin Yoshida, it includes not only documents written by loyalists from Choshu (Yamaguchi Prefecture) but also other items regardless of region or class.
