About Kyoto University Rare Materials Digital Archive

In order to contribute to academic and cultural development, Kyoto University Library Network is engaged in digitizing rare materials our libraries hold and making them available online. We now have one of the largest digital collection in Japan: as of June, 2026, the number of digitized items reached more than 2.3 million images of over 27,000 titles, including Konjaku monogatari-shu (Suzuka Ed.), designated as a national treasure, and important cultural properties.

Kyoto University Rare Materials Digital Archive (KURare) was newly released in September 2017. The system has viewers enabling the smooth display of high-definition images, and is compliant with the International Image Interoperability Framework (IIIF). We will be continuously transferring the data from the previous system to this new system, while examining and implementing methods of data publication using the features of IIIF, in order to facilitate the use of digitized images in our collection.

* 2,313,369 images of 27,626 titles are available in this digital archive as of Sept 17, 2026.
* Historical materials in the digital archive may include expressions that are considered inappropriate in modern society. We publicize such images as they are in order to preserve the authenticity of the original materials.
