Ongoing collections search issues

		
We are aware of the ongoing issues with our Collections Search and are working with IT Services to find solutions.


If your search results are not as expected, or if your search returns a blank page, please contact IT Support at itservicedesk@st-andrews.ac.uk, and they will look into the issue as soon as possible.

	



  

    

      

        

        	
     			
        	
  
        

        

            

                
University Collections at St Andrews brings together archives, rare books, photographic, art and museum collections. These active collections are:


    

  • used for teaching and research
    • 

  • consulted in a dedicated research room
    • 

  • digitised and made accessible online
    • 

  • displayed in exhibitions in St Andrews and elsewhere. 
    • 



The University Collections search does not currently include rare books. Search the library to find information on rare books.
