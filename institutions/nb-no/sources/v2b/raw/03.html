Description

NB DH-LAB is a corpus infrastructure for social sciences and humanities computing. The infrastructure provides researchers with methods for doing qualitative and quantitative analyses of the digital texts from the vast digital collections of the National Library of Norway. The digital collections comprise more than 160 billion words for Norwegian, making them one of the largest corpora worldwide.

  • Landing page: https://dh.nb.no
  • REST API: https://api.nb.no/dhlab
  • Python package: https://pypi.org/project/dhlab/
  • Documentation: https://dhlab.readthedocs.io/en/stable/
  • R package: https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dhlabR
  • Web applications: https://dh.nb.no/apps
